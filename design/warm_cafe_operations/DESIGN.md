## Brand & Style

### Personality & Purpose
The design system is crafted specifically for cafe owners and solo operators who balance daily floor service with operational management. The brand persona is calm, reliable, tactile, and highly organized—evoking the quiet precision of an artisanal coffee counter after peak hours. It departs from cold, generic enterprise software by embracing an organic, warm-neutral palette while maintaining strict tabular clarity.

### Target Emotional Response
- **Reassurance:** Numbers, margins, and inventory shortages are legible without causing cognitive overload.
- **Warm Efficiency:** Interactions feel smooth, quiet, and grounded, lowering stress during late-night closing audits.
- **Professional Dignity:** Gives solo entrepreneurs the analytical confidence of enterprise tooling packaged with the warmth of a boutique cafe.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
- **Warm Minimalist SaaS:** A tailored union of contemporary utilitarian dashboard structure and artisanal cafe aesthetics.
- **Physical Texture Metaphors:** Clean cream-tinted paper substrates, roasted espresso borders, and subtle earthy accents instead of stark blue-tinted corporate planes.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette avoids synthetic cool-grays and stark white voids, using authentic coffee and botanical pigments to establish focus, trust, and fatigue-free screen time.

- **Primary (`#4A3525` - Roasted Espresso):** Drives key calls to action, active navigation tabs, and principal brand identifiers.
- **Secondary (`#2E5339` - Forest Sage):** Signals profit margins, healthy stock levels, positive revenue variances, and completed prep batches.
- **Tertiary (`#C47B2B` - Warm Amber):** Highlights pending tasks, approaching reorder thresholds, and draft closing states.
- **Neutral (`#766F68` - Warm Slate):** Used for structural iconography, supporting text, and secondary interface chrome.

### Surface Hierarchy & Contrast
- **Canvas (`surface-cream` / `#FAF8F5`):** Soft, non-glare cream ground resembling fine kraft stationery.
- **Containers (`surface-cream-raised` / `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for active data tables, ledger cards, and modal forms.
- **Recessed Areas (`surface-cream-sunken` / `#F2EDE4`):** Subtly tinted container for table headers, inactive tabs, and segmented switch tracks.
- **Text Primary (`text-charcoal` / `#262422`):** Delivers WCAG AAA contrast against cream surfaces while softening optical harshness compared to pure `#000000`.

## Typography

### Structural Choices
- **Primary Interface Family (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers balanced humanism and clear letterforms, avoiding clinical dryness while ensuring dense financial cards remain instantly scannable.
- **Numerical & Ledger Role (JetBrains Mono):** Dedicated to numeric metric inputs, raw stock unit weights (`g`, `ml`), prices, and cost margins. Monospaced alignment prevents table figures from shifting and wobbling across columns during multi-row auditing.

### Application Rules
- Apply `data-tabular-*` styles exclusively to currency amounts, batch ratios, recipe proportions, and inventory quantiles.
- Headings use negative letter-spacing for sharp, editorial authority.
- Form inputs and table headers use `label-md` and `label-sm` to maintain rigorous structure even on compact tablet screens.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Left fixed sidebar navigation (240px) with fluid analytics and table canvas.
- **Tablet / POS Display (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar collapses to an icon utility bar (64px) to maximize table columns for rapid POS end-of-day stock entry.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-stack flow with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Dense list rows replace wide financial tables, using bottom sheets for batch logs.

### Rhythm Principles
- An 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`) controls internal card architectures.
- Use `space-sm` for compact internal chip and badge padding.
- Table cell padding strictly utilizes `space-sm` vertically and `space-md` horizontally to preserve compact data density without visual clipping.

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Grounded Tonal Layers & Soft Outlines
To preserve a warm, tactile feel and prevent the screen from looking like generic floating plastic, visual elevation relies on subtle tonal planes combined with delicate warm-hairline borders (`border-warm` / `#E5DED3`).

### Elevation Levels
- **Ground (Level 0 - Surface Cream):** The base app backdrop (`#FAF8F5`). No shadow, no border.
- **Container (Level 1 - White Work Cards & Tables):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border in `#E5DED3`. Ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(74, 53, 37, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(74, 53, 37, 0.02)`.
- **Raised Interactive (Level 2 - Hovered Rows, Action Menus, Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border in `#D9CEBF`. Ambient shadow: `0 4px 14px rgba(74, 53, 37, 0.07), 0 2px 6px rgba(74, 53, 37, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays (Level 3 - Recipe Costing Modals, Reorder Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border in `#C8BEAF`. Elevated ambient drop: `0 16px 32px rgba(74, 53, 37, 0.12), 0 4px 10px rgba(74, 53, 37, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

### Geometry & Form Language
The design system adopts a balanced rounded style (`roundedness: 2`, where base radius is 8px / `0.5rem`), blending friendly cafe hospitality with the clean discipline of modern management software.

### Radius Assignments
- **Input Fields, Dropdowns & Standard Buttons:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for comfortable touch targets and visual harmony.
- **Data Cards, Metric Tiles & Table Wrap Panels:** 16px (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`) to establish distinct content islands on the warm canvas.
- **Modals & Slide-over Sheets:** 24px (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`) on exterior top corners for soft framing.
- **Status Badges, Metric Chips & Segmented Controls:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`) to emphasize their role as distinct interactive or semantic tokens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#4A3525` (Roasted Espresso), text `#FFFFFF`, border none. Hover: `#3A291C`. Active: scales down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#4A3525`, 1px border `#E5DED3`. Hover: background `#F2EDE4`.
- **Success / Confirmation Action:** Background `#2E5339`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#23412C`. Used for finalizing daily cash closings and batch deductions.

### Badges & Operational Chips
- **Actual vs. Projected:**
  - *Actual (실적):* Solid `#4A3525` text on `#F2EDE4` background.
  - *Projected (예상):* `#766F68` text, dashed 1px border `#D9CEBF`, background transparent.
- **Fulfillment Badges:**
  - *Dine-in (매장):* `#2E5339` text on `#EBF2ED` pill badge.
  - *Takeout (포장):* `#B85D19` text on `#FDF3EB` pill badge.
- **Inventory Alerts:**
  - *In Stock (정상):* `#2E5339` dot indicator + `#2E5339` label.
  - *Reorder Low (부족):* `#B85D19` badge with bold unit count.
  - *Depleted (품절/위험):* `#9E2A2B` background tinted `#FAEAEB` with high-emphasis bold text.

### Data Tables (Sales, Ledger & Stock)
- **Header:** `#F2EDE4` background, `label-sm` font, text `#6E6862`, 1px solid bottom border `#E5DED3`.
- **Row Styling:** White background, 48px height, alternating border `#FAF8F5`. Hover activates `#FBF9F6`.
- **Numeric Cells:** Set with `data-tabular-md`, right-aligned, with currency units (`원`, `VAT 제외`) set in muted body text.

### Form Inputs & Recipe Steppers
- **Text & Numeric Field:** 40px height, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E5DED3`, radius 8px, padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`. Focus ring: 2px solid `#4A3525` with 0px outline gap.
- **Unit Suffixes (`g`, `ml`, `ea`):** Baked into the trailing container zone with background `#F2EDE4` and text `#766F68`.

### Metric Cards (KPI Summaries)
- **Structure:** Level 1 elevation, 16px radius, padding `1.25rem`.
- **Layout:** Metric label in `label-sm`, value in `headline-lg` + JetBrains Mono styling, baseline comparison badge displaying percentage delta in `#2E5339` (positive) or `#9E2A2B` (negative).
